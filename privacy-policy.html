Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Seeds Season ("the App") is developed by Haiba Software. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

The App is designed to work primarily offline. We collect the following:

  • Location (approximate): When you select your country and region, this information is stored locally on your device to provide relevant crop suggestions. We do not access your GPS coordinates.
  • Garden data: Plants you add to your garden, planting dates, and growth status are stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage.
  • Language preference: Your selected language is stored locally on your device.
  • Usage data (via third parties): The App may display advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP address, and interaction data for ad serving and analytics purposes. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide personalized crop recommendations based on your selected location, season, and growing conditions.
  • To save your garden data locally so you can track your plants across sessions.
  • To display relevant advertisements (via AdMob) to support free app development.

3. Data Storage

All personal data (location, garden, preferences) is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This data is not transmitted to our servers. If you uninstall the App, all local data will be deleted.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: For displaying advertisements. AdMob may collect device information for ad personalization. See AdMob Privacy Policy.
  • Sentry: For crash reporting and performance monitoring. Sentry may collect crash logs, device info, and performance data. See Sentry Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Analytics: For usage analytics. See Google Privacy Policy.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal data to third parties, except as described in this policy (third-party services above). Aggregate, anonymized data may be used for analytics purposes.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect the information stored on your device. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights

  • Access: You can view all your data within the App (Garden tab, Settings).
  • Deletion: You can delete your garden data by removing plants individually, or by uninstalling the App.
  • Opt-out: You can limit ad tracking in your device settings (iOS: Settings > Privacy > Tracking; Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads).

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
