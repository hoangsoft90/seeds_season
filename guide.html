🌱 Seeds Season — User Guide

How to grow vegetables, herbs, and fruits on your balcony

📖 Overview

Seeds Season helps first-time growers find the easiest crops to plant right now based on your location, season, and balcony conditions. No farming experience needed!

The app supports 5 countries: Vietnam 🇻🇳, Thailand 🇹🇭, Indonesia 🇮🇩, United States 🇺🇸, and United Kingdom 🇬🇧.

🚀 Getting Started

1 Choose Your Country

When you first open the app, select the country where you live. This determines which crops and planting seasons are available.

2 Select Your Region

Pick the region or city closest to you. Different regions in the same country can have different growing conditions.

3 Set the Current Month

Choose the current month. The app uses this to know which crops are in season.

4 Choose Your Growing Location
LocationWhat It Means
🪟 WindowA windowsill with limited space and partial sun
🌿 BalconyAn open balcony with moderate space and sunlight
🌳 GardenA rooftop, yard, or open area with more space
5 Set Sunlight Hours

Estimate how many hours of direct sunlight your growing spot gets per day (2h, 4h, 6h, or 8h).

6 Set Pot Depth

Choose the depth of your pot or planter (10cm, 15cm, 20cm, or 30cm). Deeper pots allow more crop variety.

7 Get Recommendations!

Tap "Suggest Crops" and you'll get your Top 3 recommendations — the easiest crops you can grow successfully right now.

You don't need to fill in every field perfectly. The app is designed to give good suggestions even with approximate inputs.

📊 Understanding Your Results

Score & Role

Each recommended crop shows a score (higher = easier to succeed) and a role:

RoleMeaning
🌱 EasyBeginner-friendly, high success rate — start here!
📈 Step-upSlightly harder but still doable — good for your second crop

"Why" Explanation

Each crop includes a short explanation of why it was recommended, covering season fit, temperature, beginner friendliness, and space.

No Match

If the app can't find any suitable crops for your conditions, you'll see a "No Match" message. Try adjusting your pot depth, sunlight hours, or region.

📋 Crop Detail Page

Tap any crop card to see full details:

  • Timeline — Germination days and harvest window
  • Growth Stages — What happens at each phase (seedling → vegetative → harvest)
  • Optimal Conditions — Ideal temperature, sunlight, water needs, and soil type
  • Hard Constraints — Temperature limits and minimum sunlight (plants die outside these)
  • Regional Tips — Specific planting windows for your region
  • Beginner Score — How forgiving the plant is with overwatering, underwatering, and disease

🪴 My Garden

After finding crops you like, tap "Add to Garden" to start tracking them.

  • See all your planted crops at a glance
  • View planting date and current status
  • Mark a plant as "Ghost" (dead) if it didn't survive — the app learns from this for better future suggestions
Your garden data is saved locally on your device. If you uninstall the app, garden data will be lost.

🆘 First Aid

Having trouble with your plants? Use the First Aid tab:

  1. Pick a symptom — e.g., yellow leaves, wilting, spots
  2. Answer questions — The app asks a few quick questions to narrow down the cause
  3. Get a diagnosis — See what's wrong and what to do about it

🏆 Tips for Your First Successful Grow

1 Start with "Easy" crops

Follow the app's top recommendation. It picks crops with the highest chance of success for YOUR specific conditions.

2 Don't overwater

The #1 killer of beginner plants. Most balcony plants prefer slightly dry soil over soggy soil. Stick your finger 2cm into the soil — if it's dry, water; if moist, wait.

3 Match pot to plant

The app filters crops by your pot depth. Using a pot that's too shallow is the main reason crops fail. If in doubt, go deeper (20-30cm).

4 Plant in season

The app already accounts for this, but remember: planting the right crop at the right time is the biggest factor for success.

5 Track your garden

Use the Garden tab to record what you plant. Over time, the app gets smarter about what works for you.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Does the app need internet?

Most features work offline. The recommendation engine, crop data, and garden tracking all run locally. Internet is only needed for ads and crash reporting.

Is the app free?

Yes! The app is 100% free. It's supported by ads (Google AdMob).

Can I use it outside the 5 supported countries?

The app currently supports Vietnam, Thailand, Indonesia, US, and UK. Crop recommendations may still be useful for similar climates in other countries, but planting windows are region-specific.

My crop isn't growing. What do I do?

Check the 🆘 First Aid tab for common problems. Common issues: overwatering, wrong pot depth, not enough sunlight, or planting out of season.

How do I delete a plant from my garden?

In the 🪴 Garden tab, tap the plant card and select "Mark as Ghost" (dead) or remove it.
