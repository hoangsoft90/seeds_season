Privacy Policy

Trong Gi Hom Nay (Seeds Season)

Last updated: August 22, 2026

Trong Gi Hom Nay ("the App") is a free mobile application that helps users discover which crops to plant based on their location and weather conditions. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

The App collects very little personal information:

  • Location (optional): When you choose to share it, the App uses GPS coordinates to suggest crops suitable for your climate. Location data is processed locally on your device and is never stored on our servers.
  • Advertising data: The App uses Google AdMob to display ads. Google may collect device usage data, advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID), and ad interaction information as described in Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Crash reports: The App uses Sentry to log technical errors for quality improvement. This data includes device information, operating system version, and stack traces — never personal content.

2. How We Use Information

  • Location: Used solely to calculate crop recommendations matching your local climate.
  • Advertising data: Used to display relevant ads and keep the App free of charge.
  • Crash data: Used to fix bugs and improve user experience.

3. Data Storage

  • Garden data: Stored entirely on your device (AsyncStorage). We have no access to this data.
  • No user accounts: The App does not require sign-up. No personal data is sent to our servers.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Data may be shared with:

  • Google AdMob: For displaying ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.): For tracking technical errors. See Sentry's Privacy Policy.

5. Your Rights

  • Revoke location access: Go to Device Settings → Apps → Trong Gi Hom Nay → Location → Deny.
  • Delete data: Uninstalling the App removes all garden data from your device.
  • Personalized ads: Go to Google Settings → Ads → Opt out of personalized ads.

6. Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 years of age (or the equivalent age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with the latest update date.
